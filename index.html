Application AI

A personal job-search assistant for the Mac, used by one person.

Application AI looks for job postings in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, writes a CV and a cover letter for each suitable posting from its user's own profile, fills in the employer's online application form, and keeps track of every application.

With its user's permission it connects to her Gmail account in order to:

  • read replies from employers and match them to her applications,
  • recognise applications she sent herself, so that no employer gets an application twice,
  • read the confirmation codes that employers' career portals send when she signs in,
  • send job applications by email and notification emails from her own address.

The app runs on her own Mac. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.
