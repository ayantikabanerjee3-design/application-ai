Application AI: privacy policy

Application AI is a personal job-search app that runs on its user's own Mac. This page explains what it does with data from her Google account. About the app

What the app accesses

  • Reading her Gmail (gmail.readonly): replies from employers, to update the status of her applications; applications in her Sent folder, so that the app never applies twice to the same job; confirmation codes and links that employers' career portals send when she signs in; and replies she sends to the app's own notifications to approve an application.
  • Sending email (gmail.send): job applications she asked the app to send, and notification emails to the addresses she chose. Every email goes out from her own address.

Where the data is kept

Only on her Mac: what the app needs for her application tracker (sender, subject, date, category and a short summary of relevant emails) is stored in a local database, and the Gmail sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain. There is no server and no cloud storage of her Google data.

Who else receives it

  • To sort an email and to draft a reply, the app sends the email's sender, subject and text to the OpenAI API, which returns the result to the app.
  • If she turns on WhatsApp notifications, short messages (for example "Reply from an employer") go to her own phone through the CallMeBot service.

Her Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Application AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

"Disconnect Gmail" in the app's settings deletes the sign-in token. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app together with its data folder removes everything it stored.
